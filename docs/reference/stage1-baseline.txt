1 Executive Summary 
The proposed One-Person AI Company modernises a cosmetics/skincare WeChat commerce 
prototype. One Human Founder / Operator supervises three AI business roles: Beauty Advisor (BA), 
Customer Service and Order Agent (CS), and Marketing Content Agent (MC). They provide grounded 
recommendations, bounded after-sales handling and reviewable promotional-video drafts. Approval 
and Governance (AP) and Coordination and Recovery (CO) are shared controls. 
Five requirement/use-case chains connect features, structure, runtime and behaviour. Deterministic 
evidence, authority and persistence checks constrain large language model (LLM) proposals; 
clarification, failure, uncertain execution and pending review are explicit outcomes. The proof of 
concept (PoC) uses controlled mocks and excludes production settlement and autonomous public 
posting.

2 Project Background and Scope 
2.1 Problem and meaningful improvement 
A small beauty retailer needs product advice, routine after-sales support and marketing material 
under one accountable operator. The existing mini-program supplies feasibility evidence, but does 
not establish requirements or demonstrate the proposed agent behaviour. 
The improvement combines specialised agents, traceable hand-offs and recovery. Agents interpret 
requests and generate explanations/drafts; controlled services enforce evidence, authority and 
human checkpoints. This coordinated workflow provides the meaningful improvement over the legacy 
shop.

Related requirements: BA-M-01; BA-FR-01/02/03/04; BA-NFR-01/02/03/04; optional BA-O-01. 
Authority: Read authorised catalogue/stock and relevant Customer input; record consultation and 
minimal audit/review context; advise the requesting Customer. No orders, payments, refunds, 
inventory/price changes or public posting. Product selection is not purchase authorisation. 
Preconditions: Customer access and service interfaces/labelled mocks are configured. Successful 
service responses are checked during the flow. 
Trigger: The Customer submits a beauty consultation request containing a goal, preference or 
constraint. 
Main success scenario  
1. The Customer submits the request. 
2. Create/resume the consultation and record relevant input. 
3. Extract needs; clarify missing information through A1. 
4. Retrieve authorised catalogue evidence. 
5. Check availability against the retrieved product IDs. 
6. Propose candidates with reasons and supporting evidence. 
7. Validate product IDs, constraints, facts and availability; unsupported output follows E3. 
8. Save the validated result; present at least one available recommendation with evidence-backed 
reasons and limitations. 
9. Customer reviews the advice; consultation success ends here. Purchasing requires a separate 
Customer-initiated workflow. 
Alternative flows  
A1 at step 3: Ask a focused question and return to step 3. If Customer declines, record an incomplete 
outcome without guessing. 
A2 at steps 4–5: If no product matches, invite revised needs and return to step 3; otherwise record a 
safe incomplete outcome. 
A3 at step 5: With BA-F-03 ON and Customer requesting alternatives, retrieve fresh evidence, 
validate alternatives and explain differences; otherwise follow A2. 
A4: Customer correction invalidates the prior recommendation and requires re-evaluation. 
Cancellation stops processing, suppresses late output and is recorded. 
Failure and uncertainty flows  
E1: Catalogue/stock error, timeout or missing availability produces an explicit incomplete outcome. 
Do not invent stock; Customer-initiated retry refreshes both sources. 
E2: Stop diagnosis/treatment advice; Founder review cannot override this medical boundary. 
E3: Withhold failed, malformed, unsupported or contradictory LLM output, or advice not justified by 
evidence. Clarify, seek human review or remain incomplete; do not invent a confidence threshold. 
E4: With Customer consent, submit minimal context and the issue for Founder review. 
REVIEW_PENDING requires an acknowledged receipt; unavailable review is not submitted. 
Rejection or no decision grants no approval. Later advice repeats fresh validation; pending review is 
not success. 
E5: Required recording failure remains incomplete. Deliberate retry creates no financial effect. 
Success postcondition: Available, validated advice is delivered; input, evidence, stock observation 
and outcome are recorded. 
Failure postcondition: Unsupported, cancelled or pending advice is not success; no order/unrelated 
write occurs. Record the reason when storage is available; otherwise expose recording failure. 
Data used or changed: Relevant input, optional authorised profile subset, product/stock evidence, 
recommendation, outcome and minimal audit/review context. Retention remains unagreed.

7 Architecture Analysis and Design 
7.1 Drivers principles and viewpoints 
The event-driven design separates three specialised business services through ports/adapters. 
Deterministic logic checks identity, evidence, policy, scope and persistence; LLMs propose 
language/content. Adapters distinguish live/mock results and expose failures. Revisions suppress 
stale results; human review is durable. 
Viewpoint Evidence and design question 
Logical GR-CL-01 and complete member class slices: which entities, ownership, 
associations and receiver contracts realise each requirement? 
Process and runtime GR-S-01, object/composite/communication and individual behaviour models: how do 
work, decisions, uncertainty and recording progress? 
Development Presentation, application/domain services and provider adapters: how can live and 
controlled mock components be replaced without changing authority? 
Scenario Five use cases and GR-S-01 expose both success and safe incomplete paths; 
Section 13 links acceptance evidence. 
Physical deployment Not selected as a formal deployment model. Hosting technology and production 
availability/access guarantees remain open.

CS: eligibility/actionRef/reconciliation. MC: grounded media/exact-draft review. AP: commit versus 
delivery. CO: acknowledgement versus outcome/recovery.

13.2 Change assessment basis 
Action/context changes affect requirements/use cases, evidence, Customer confirmation, review 
envelopes and dependent classes/interaction/state guards. Revised MC media/variants need fresh 
exact-scope review; CO/CS reassess authority and uncertain effects. Preserve history without 
inheriting permission.

Section 13.3 supplies regression specifications.
